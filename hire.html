FAQ

Common questions

How quickly can engineers start?

After a short discovery conversation we confirm scope, the team composition and a start date. Focused engagements can begin quickly; larger teams are assembled in stages.

Can we start small and grow the team?

Yes. Many engagements start with one engineer or a specialist and expand into a pod or a complete team as the product grows.

Do you work with our existing team?

Our engineers work inside your repositories, tools and rituals, or deliver independently and hand over with documentation, whichever suits the engagement.

Who owns the code and infrastructure?

You do. We deploy into your cloud and your accounts, with documented deployments and source code you own.

Which time zones do you cover?

We are based in India (IST) and work with clients across India, the UK and Europe, with overlapping working hours for collaboration.

How are engagements priced?

Dedicated engineers and pods are custom engagements; product development is a scoped proposal; AI and infrastructure work is priced from the architecture. Ask for a proposal and we'll put the options side by side.
