How it runs

The risk sits with us, and you see the progress

01

Architecture & demo first

You sign off a full system design, then see a working demo of the core flow on staging before any production code is written.
02

Milestone-gated payments

Fixed scope and fixed price. Payments are tied to accepted deliverables, not hours.
03

A written report every week

What shipped, what's next and what's at risk. It takes two minutes to read, and there are no status meetings.
04

Scope freeze after sign-off

New ideas are welcome. They become costed changes rather than silent scope creep that wrecks the timeline.
05

"Critical bug" is defined in writing

P0: the system is down. P1: data loss or cross-tenant data exposure. Agreeing these definitions up front keeps "bug" from becoming a billing argument.
06

Handover you can run

Architecture docs, a runbook, a walkthrough with your team and a period of post-launch support. Your cloud costs and third-party API accounts (telephony, STT, LLM) stay in your name.

What we're not

  • Not hyperscale. We build for startups through mid-market.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AIM?

AIM (AI Infrastructure & Machines), also known as AIM Studio, is a Udyam-registered business in Madhya Pradesh, India, with Ankit Panicker as CTO & AI Systems Engineer. It makes APEX Connect (SaaS) and AIM (a free AI operating layer), and it takes on B2B engineering engagements.

How is APEX Connect priced?

Per tenant, per month: ₹4,999 with your own SIP carrier, or an all-inclusive calling plan at ₹14,999 for 2,000 AI calls, ₹29,999 for 4,500 or ₹49,999 for 10,000. Business, Enterprise and White-label are custom. GST applies where relevant.

Which carriers and languages are supported?

Twilio, Telnyx, Vapi and Indian SIP trunk providers, plus the WhatsApp Business API. Calls are supported in English and Hindi.

What does "outside IR35" mean?

The engagement is a genuine business-to-business contract: fixed scope and milestone payments, the contractor carries the financial risk and provides their own tools, and there's no mutuality of obligation. You contract directly with AIM, a Udyam-registered business in India.

What is end-to-end product delivery?

AIM takes the product from architecture and system design through backend and frontend engineering, infrastructure (Terraform, Kubernetes), CI/CD and monitoring to production deployment, staffed with one engineer or a full team depending on the scope.

Can I hire one engineer or a whole team?

Yes. Hire a single dedicated engineer for a focused build, review or rescue, or a team sized to the scope for a full platform. The CTO stays accountable for the architecture either way.

Is AIM (Remote AI) really free?

Yes. It's free for Windows, Android and Chrome with no account. You bring a local model or your own cloud provider key.
