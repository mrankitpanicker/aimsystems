Process

From idea to production, owned end to end.

The same four steps on every engagement, in this order.

01 · UNDERSTAND

What should exist, what can fail and what we should deliberately not build.

02 · DESIGN

The architecture, data model, failure modes and threat boundaries, signed off before production code.

03 · BUILD

Backend, frontend, workers, queues and AI pipelines, tested against the failure path as well as the happy path.

04 · DEPLOY & SCALE

CI/CD, observability, runbooks and handover, so the system can be operated, recovered and extended.
