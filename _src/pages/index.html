AIM: an AI operating layer for your own machines (free)

AIM runs on your Windows PC and connects your phone, desktop and voice to it, so you can see, control and hand work to that machine. It works with local models or with OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, OpenRouter and Groq. The connection is a direct, end-to-end encrypted WebRTC link, and you don't need an account.

APEX HMS: offline-first hospital infrastructure

A multi-tenant hospital management platform for OPD/IPD, beds, pharmacy, lab, billing and notifications. It uses a write-ahead log, replay, durable outboxes and tamper-evident audit chains so it stays usable when the network doesn't. It is still under active construction.

Shortz: a local AI video pipeline

Script → TTS → voice alignment → subtitles → FFmpeg render, using FastAPI, Redis, XTTS, Whisper, Prometheus and Grafana. Every stage records its own timing, and the pipeline has soak tests and failure injection.

04 · Process

From idea to production, with one owner.

01 · UNDERSTAND

What should exist, what can fail and what we should deliberately not build.

02 · DESIGN

The architecture, data model, failure modes and threat boundaries, signed off before production code.

03 · BUILD

Backend, frontend, workers, queues and AI pipelines, tested against the failure path as well as the happy path.

04 · DEPLOY & SCALE

CI/CD, observability, runbooks and handover, so the system can be operated, recovered and extended.
