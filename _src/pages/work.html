Apple Hospital: patient outreach on APEX Connect

In production
1 · Client

Apple Hospital · Healthcare · India · in production.

2 · Business problem

Reaching large patient lists for outreach while keeping the operational workload on hospital teams manageable.

3 · Constraints
  • · Multi-tenant platform shared with other institutions
  • · Per-tenant capacity limits
  • · Multiple communication providers
  • · Voice and WhatsApp in one flow
4 · Architecture · campaign flow

Patient list → Validation → Campaign queue → Voice workers → Retry engine → WhatsApp follow-up → Outcome reporting
  1. Patient list — the hospital uploads the outreach list.
  2. Validation — numbers are checked before anything is dialled.
  3. Campaign queue — calls wait within the tenant's capacity limit.
  4. Voice workers — background workers place the calls.
  5. Retry engine — unanswered numbers are retried on a schedule.
  6. WhatsApp follow-up — a message reaches those the call didn't.
  7. Outcome reporting — every result lands in an automated report.

  • · Architecture
  • · Backend workers
  • · Telephony and WhatsApp integration
  • · Retry scheduling
  • · Tenant isolation
  • · Deployment and production operation
