Privacy Policy

This website. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores your theme and sound preferences locally on your device; they are not sent to us.

Project briefs. The contact form does not store anything on this site. Submitting it opens your own email application with the brief filled in, and we receive only what you choose to send.

Job applications. When you apply through our careers page, we store your application details and CV so we can review them. We use them only for recruitment.

The website assistant. The chat assistant answers from content on this site and runs entirely in your browser. Questions you type are not sent to us or to any third party.

Your rights. You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the business email on this page.

Data Processing

In client engagements, our clients decide what data a system processes and why; we process it on their behalf and according to their instructions. Where a client needs a data processing agreement, we put one in place as part of the engagement.

Systems are deployed into the client's preferred cloud environment where possible, using the client's own provider accounts and credentials, so the data stays under the client's control.

Data Retention

We keep project correspondence for as long as needed to deliver and support an engagement, and job applications for as long as needed to complete recruitment. Data processed for clients is retained according to the client's agreement and configuration, and is returned or deleted at the end of the engagement on request.

Subprocessors

Depending on the service, we use the following categories of providers:

  • Website hosting and storage: Google Firebase (hosting, application records) and Cloudflare (CV file storage).
  • Cloud infrastructure: the client's chosen provider, such as Microsoft Azure, AWS or Google Cloud.
  • Communication: telephony providers (such as Twilio, Telnyx, Vapi or SIP carriers) and the WhatsApp Business API.
  • AI processing: speech-to-text, language model and text-to-speech providers selected for each deployment, or models run locally.

The providers used in a specific engagement are listed in that engagement's documentation.

AI Transparency

Our AI systems combine speech recognition, language models and speech synthesis with business rules and tools defined by the client. Clients configure how AI agents introduce themselves, what they can do and when a conversation is handed to a person.

We design AI features to be observable: interactions can be logged, reviewed and audited by the client, and models and providers can be changed without rebuilding the system.

Security

Systems we build use authentication and authorization, tenant isolation, encryption in transit (TLS), scoped access, managed secrets, audit logging, rate limiting, monitoring, backups and recovery procedures, applied as appropriate to each system. Read more about our practices.

To report a security concern, contact us at our business email.

Terms

Engagements. Client engagements are business-to-business agreements covering scope, deliverables, timelines and payment, agreed in writing before work begins.

Ownership. Unless agreed otherwise, clients own the source code, documentation and infrastructure delivered for them.

Platforms. Use of APEX Connect and other AIM platforms is governed by the agreement or terms provided with that service. Usage-based charges depend on call duration, communication provider and AI processing requirements.
