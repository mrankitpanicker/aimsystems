FAQ

UK & EU questions

Which hours do you work?

We are in India (IST, UTC+5:30), 4½ hours ahead of the UK in summer and 5½ in winter, and 3½–4½ hours ahead of central Europe. Meetings, reviews and support run in your business hours.

Who do we sign the contract with?

With AIM — AI Infrastructure & Machines, a Udyam-registered company in India. It is a business-to-business services agreement; you do not hire or pay individuals.

How is our data handled?

We work inside your cloud accounts and your chosen region, with access you grant and can revoke. Where personal data is involved we work under your data-processing terms. See our privacy and compliance page for details.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. Code lives in your repositories from the first commit, deployments are documented, and credentials stay under your control.

How are engagements priced and invoiced?

Dedicated engineers and pods are monthly capacity; product work is a scoped proposal; AI and infrastructure work is priced from the architecture. Currency and payment terms are agreed in the contract.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. Our engineers join your repositories, tools and rituals, or own a workstream end to end and hand over with documentation, whichever suits you.

Need engineers who overlap your day?

Tell us what you're building. You'll get a team proposal with composition, start date and pricing.
