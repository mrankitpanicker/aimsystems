## Engagements
1. Build — a new product or platform: architecture → engineering → production. First deliverable: architecture document and delivery plan.
2. Fix — rescue an existing system: architecture review → reliability → performance → modernization. First deliverable: risk map and a stabilisation plan.
3. Integrate — connect complex systems: AI, APIs, telephony, WhatsApp, CRMs, workflows, infrastructure. First deliverable: integration design and a working path end to end.
4. Operate — take ownership of production: observability, reliability, optimisation, ongoing engineering. First deliverable: monitoring, alerting and a runbook.
Team shape: one senior engineer, a specialist, an engineering pod or a complete team. Architecture review: bring the current architecture, bottleneck or production problem; AIM identifies the highest-risk parts and recommends the shortest path forward.
Pricing: dedicated engineers and pods are custom engagements; product development is a scoped proposal; AI/infrastructure work is architecture-based.
